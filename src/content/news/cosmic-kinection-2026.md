Safe Journey Sanctum provided 24/7 sanctuary support at Cosmic Kinection 2026 in Astral Valley from Thursday, June 4 through the morning of Sunday, June 7.

The deployment brought a calm, lower-stimulation space, trained peer presence, harm-reduction education, practical supplies when stocked, and clear coordination with event safety teams. The team stayed available across the festival's nights and mornings so guests could find support whenever they needed a quieter place or a next step.

Cosmic Kinection 2026 is part of SJS's growing field experience. Future recaps will continue to share verified deployment details, volunteer learning, and improvements that strengthen the sanctuary model from one event to the next.
