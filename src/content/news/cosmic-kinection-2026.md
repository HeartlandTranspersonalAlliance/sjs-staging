Safe Journey Sanctum provided 24/7 sanctuary support at Cosmic Kinection 2026 in Astral Valley from Thursday, June 4 through the morning of Sunday, June 7.

Trained volunteers staffed a quieter space through the festival's days and nights. Guests could ask for peer support, harm reduction information or supplies when stocked. The team coordinated with event safety staff when someone needed more help.

In future recaps, we’ll share deployment details, what volunteers learned and changes to the sanctuary from one event to the next.

If you are planning an event, [contact SJS about partnership support](../../contact/). If you want to help staff future deployments, [apply to volunteer](https://forms.gle/iZt6DJF9YRQfXHHx5).
